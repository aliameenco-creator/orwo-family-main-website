Our Heritage

100+ Years of Film Excellence

Founded in Wolfen in 1909 as part of AGFA, ORWO (an abbreviation of ORiginal WOlfen) has been synonymous with photographic and cinematic film for over a century. In 1936, the Wolfen plant developed the world’s first modern color film (Agfacolor), a full year before Kodak released Kodachrome. Throughout the mid-20th century, ORWO emerged as East Germany’s flagship film producer—and a major exporter to Russia, Poland, India, Southeast Asia, and other global markets. Renowned for its black-and-white films and technological breakthroughs, the division of Germany after WWII saw the Wolfen factory continue under the ORWO name, supplying much of the Eastern Bloc’s photographic film needs.

Over the decades, ORWO accumulated numerous patents and innovation awards, even earning a place in the Guinness Book of Records for its technical achievements. Film manufacturing at the Wolfen site has been a continuous tradition since 1910, making ORWO one of the longest-running names in analog film. While the ORWO corporate entity underwent restructurings in the 1990s, the brand’s core expertise lived on—carried by successor companies and now resurrected under the ORWO Family. In 2020, the film coating firm Inoviscoat (with its AGFA heritage) merged with ORWO’s film production arm (Filmotec GmbH) under common ownership, reuniting historic emulsions and know-how under the ORWO banner.

This careful stewardship of analog film IP means today’s ORWO Family can proudly claim a 116+ year lineage of film R&D and quality, even as the organization itself has evolved and continues to evolve. We acknowledge this unique history openly: ORWO Family stands on the shoulders of its predecessors, combining the legacy of AGFA Wolfen and original ORWO with a modern corporate structure that includes a deeply experienced board that has a combined celluloid experience of more than 1,000 years.

"Supplying archival film to the U.S. Library of Congress, MoMA, and the Smithsonian isn’t just a privilege—it’s a testament to the enduring quality of ORWO’s legacy and chemistry."

Notably, ORWO’s expertise in archival and specialty films remains highly respected worldwide. Our black-and-white cinema films and archival stock are trusted by institutions seeking permanence and fidelity. This deep root in film technology forms the bedrock of ORWO’s credibility as we expand into new ventures.

Innovation in Film

New Products and Technologies

Far from simply trading on nostalgia, ORWO Family is pioneering new technologies in analog film, green energy, archiving, print delivery, cameras and studio infrastructure. We are a science-and-craft enterprise building the future. In 2025, we are released two newly developed film stocks alongside innovations in portable processing and long-term data storage:

NC200

WOLFEN NC200 (Color, 200 ASA)

debuted July 2025, this color negative film stock is a contemporary remake of a vintage AGFA-era recipe. With a “blank canvas” neutral tone, it balances analog richness with consistent results. It is suitable for C-41 or ECN-2 workflows, enabling hybrid still/motion lab applications.

NP400

WOLFEN NP400 (B&W, 400 ASA)

a wide-latitude panchromatic film with ultra-fine grain, this high-speed black-and-white emulsion is one of the most ambitious B&W launches since the 1990s. Shot from 160 to 1600 ISO with deep shadow detail, it’s already gaining cult status among street photographers and experimental filmmakers.

NC500 · NC400

These films build on the success of NC500 and NC400, which brought cinematic color palettes and experimental ISO curves into the market in 2022–2023. All films are made in Germany using modernized AGFA/ORWO coating technology.

In 2025, we are released two newly developed film stocks alongside innovations in portable processing and long-term data storage. In Q1 2026 we are launching a suite of a dozen 65mm cameras as we look to perfect our first 65mm film stock release; as well as a mobile feature film lab now capable of 16mm and 35mm processing and scanning (on site), with the overall goal of making film faster than digital.

R+D work is successfully completed on a 4K direct to print technology, with commercialisation set for 2027.

Meanwhile the latest ION Power system (purely green tech) is being real world tested in April 2026 and is expected to deliver 750 kw/h, base load 24/7/365.

“From jungle sets to arctic fields—film doesn’t wait. Now, it doesn’t have to.”

Beyond camera films, ORWO is investing in critical infrastructure for archiving: Paper to Film (PTF), Audio Visual to Film (AVTF), Data to Film (DTF)

These formats create unhackable, light-readable, chemically stable archives. Film—unlike digital—is futureproof. Under ideal conditions, these films have been tested to last up to 500 years.

LOVE OF RACE

A multiple time winner of Rally races. ORWO was famous for supplying the race team with innovative materials for the windshields and chassis - giving them a technological advantage.

The passionate support for innovation, technical challenges and the race for perfection continued throughout the 70s, in Formula 3. ORWO was universally recognised as a pioneering force.

ORWO's twin passions of social responsibility and fast fast cars combined in the 80s. Formula Easter cars became famous throughout the world racing in the Peace and Friendship cup. The Formula Easters were known as the Eastern Bloc Formula Ones.
